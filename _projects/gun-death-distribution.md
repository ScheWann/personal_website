In this visualization, the map is split into two sections representing states and counties, giving a nuanced view of
how gun deaths are distributed across these geographic divisions.

A packed bubble chart carries the large-county data, while a stacked bar chart provides detail for each state. Selecting a state
drives an interactive scatter plot that breaks down male and female victims, adding a layer of exploration on top of the
overview.
