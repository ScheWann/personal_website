Designed and implemented a web crawler with Selenium that collected the 2016 "resident life" fiscal data for all 34
provinces in China and loaded it automatically into MySQL. For the handful of provinces whose fiscal data was published in an
unusual format, BeautifulSoup4 handled the extraction and storage directly.
