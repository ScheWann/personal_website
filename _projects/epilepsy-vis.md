To improve the diagnosis and management of patients with severe epilepsy, it is essential to accurately detect
epileptic seizures from EEG signals recorded across many electrodes. This project introduces a visual analysis tool that helps
physicians determine both the origin of epileptic activity and the intracranial pathways along which it propagates.
