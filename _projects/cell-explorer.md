This work provides deeper insight into the molecular mechanisms underlying complex biological processes within
tissues. The design couples a part-to-whole circular layout with linked views so analysts can move between individual cells and
the tissue-level context without losing their place.

It was accepted to the [IEEE VIS 2024 Bio+Med+Vis Challenge](https://ieeevis.org/year/2024/program/paper_a-biomedchallenge-9833.html) workshop {% cite zhao2024parttowhole %}.
